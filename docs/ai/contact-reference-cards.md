# Governed contact reference cards

## Purpose

Contact reference cards help a visitor understand who owns a request and how to continue through a channel that has publication consent. They are not a directory-discovery feature and never infer a person, role, language, schedule, or service level.

## Current approved card

The current directory contains one department-level card for Glitz Pride project enquiries. It uses the separately approved WhatsApp destination governed by `KS-004` and `APR-004`.

The card displays:

- Department name: Glitz Pride project enquiries
- Role: Project enquiry coordinator
- Channel: user-reviewed WhatsApp message
- Languages: Not confirmed
- Working hours: Not confirmed
- Response time: Not confirmed
- Source label, approval record, and review date

No individual name or photograph is displayed because neither is approved for publication.

## Withheld information

The brochure-listed phone and email records do not create professional contact cards. `KS-018` remains pending and prohibits treating those records as a current professional directory. The application therefore withholds:

- personal names and portraits;
- direct phone and email cards;
- team language claims;
- working hours and availability;
- response-time or delivery promises; and
- specialist ownership that has not been approved.

If a requested channel has no approved card, the preview identifies that channel as unavailable instead of substituting an unapproved contact.

## Interaction boundary

Opening the WhatsApp action produces a neutral draft for the visitor to review. It does not include conversation history, form fields, contact details, dates, villa preferences, or inferred information. Nothing is sent automatically, and opening the action does not prove receipt or availability.

## Approval workflow

Future cards require a structured record with the professional or department name, role, approved channels, supported languages, working hours, publication consent, source reference, review date, and approval owner. Photographs require separate publication rights. Any missing field remains explicitly unknown.
