# Visual Direction — Quiet Tropical Modernism

**Task:** T0006 — Create visual direction  
**Owner:** AKHIL  
**Status:** Completed  
**Prepared:** 3 September 2026  
**Concept:** Quiet Tropical Modernism

## 1. Creative idea

Present the community as a calm, private place where contemporary architecture belongs to the Kerala landscape. The experience should feel editorial and architectural rather than like a conventional property portal: fewer competing messages, stronger imagery, tactile materials, spacious layouts, and evidence placed close to every important claim.

The existing website provides project context and temporary imagery. The new visual language is original and must not reproduce its layout, graphics, or styling.

## 3. Design principles

### Architecture leads

Use large, carefully cropped architectural imagery and concise copy. Interface decoration must support the property rather than compete with it.

### Nature is structural

Green is not an accent applied at the end. Landscape, daylight, natural materials, botanical detail, and breathing space form the visual system.

### Luxury through restraint

Create value through proportion, typography, texture, motion, and craft. Avoid glossy gold treatments, crowded statistic strips, excessive shadows, and promotional urgency.

### Evidence feels premium

Source dates, status labels, specifications, plan references, and verified details should be elegant and easy to find. Trust is part of the aesthetic.

### Mobile is a composed experience

Mobile screens should preserve the editorial rhythm, imagery, information, and actions—not collapse into an endless stack of unrelated cards.

## 5. Typography

### Display — Cormorant Garamond

Use for hero statements, major section headings, and occasional editorial quotations. Its warm, classical character adds distinction without imitating a corporate luxury template.

- Hero: 64–88 px desktop, 44–56 px tablet, 38–46 px mobile
- Section heading: 44–64 px desktop, 34–44 px tablet, 30–38 px mobile
- Recommended weights: 500 and 600
- Use tight but readable line height and avoid all-uppercase paragraphs

### Interface and body — Manrope

Use for navigation, body copy, facts, forms, buttons, labels, captions, and data. It provides clean contrast against the expressive display face.

- Body large: 18–20 px
- Body: 16–18 px
- Supporting text: 14–16 px
- Labels: 12–14 px with restrained letter spacing
- Recommended weights: 400, 500, 600, and 700

Use system fallbacks and self-hosted or framework-managed font delivery. Subset fonts and avoid unnecessary weights.

## 6. Layout and spacing

- Maximum editorial content width: 1440 px
- Reading-width copy: 620–720 px
- Desktop horizontal page padding: 56–88 px
- Tablet horizontal padding: 32–48 px
- Mobile horizontal padding: 20–24 px
- Base spacing unit: 4 px
- Common section rhythm: 96–144 px desktop, 72–96 px tablet, 56–72 px mobile
- Prefer asymmetric 7/5 or 8/4 compositions for story sections
- Use full-bleed imagery selectively; alternate with contained editorial sections
- Keep action groups compact and visually connected to the information they affect

## 7. Image direction

### Preferred subjects

- Villa elevations at human eye level
- Shaded entrances and thresholds
- Courtyards, balconies, and framed garden views
- Tropical planting and mature tree canopy
- Natural daylight, ventilation, and indoor-outdoor transitions
- Tactile details: timber, stone, plaster, metal, fabric, and landscape
- Community paths and shared spaces at a believable scale
- Family lifestyle only when consent, usage rights, and authenticity are clear

### Treatment

- Favor warm daylight with natural greens and controlled contrast
- Maintain realistic materials and avoid oversaturated foliage
- Use 16:9 or 3:2 for cinematic storytelling, 4:5 for editorial cards, and square crops only where functionally useful
- Provide art-directed mobile crops when the focal point would otherwise be lost
- Avoid heavy gradients; apply a restrained directional overlay only when text sits on imagery
- Never present generated concept imagery as documentary evidence of a completed villa or amenity

## 8. Signature visual motifs

### The threshold frame

Use architectural portal-like frames around key imagery or content to evoke entry, privacy, and transition without drawing literal building outlines everywhere.

### The landscape line

Use thin organic or gently stepped rules inspired by terrain and pathways to connect section labels, plan references, and navigation states.

### Material cards

Use flat, tactile surfaces with fine borders instead of floating glass panels. Corners may be subtly rounded, but the system should feel architectural rather than playful.

### Evidence tags

Show labels such as Verified, Proposed, Last updated, or Source available as quiet, consistent metadata near the relevant content.

## 10. Homepage art direction

### Hero

Use one cinematic architectural image with an image-aware overlay. Place a short statement and Explore Villas action in a controlled editorial block. Keep the navigation quiet and the Schedule a Visit action available without dominating the story.

### Story transition

Move from a full visual opening into an ivory editorial section containing a short project narrative and one verified fact cluster. Use a landscape or material detail as the visual counterpoint.

### Villa discovery

Use a horizontal editorial sequence on larger screens and a composed vertical sequence on mobile. Cards should invite detail exploration rather than imitate listing-marketplace tiles.

### Amenities and plan

Pair amenity imagery with a simplified plan preview. The user should understand that the community is a connected place, not a collection of unrelated facilities.

### Conversion close

End with a warm, credible invitation to visit, supported by location context and transparent contact options. Avoid artificial scarcity and countdown messaging.

## 11. Motion direction

- Use motion to reveal spatial relationships and focus, not for spectacle
- Standard interface transition: 160–240 ms
- Editorial reveal: 400–700 ms with small distance and gentle easing
- Image scale changes must remain subtle, approximately 1.00 to 1.03
- Avoid scroll hijacking, continuous parallax, autoplay audio, and motion that delays access to information
- Honor reduced-motion preferences by removing transforms and using immediate or opacity-only state changes
- Preserve focus and screen-reader announcements during drawers, dialogs, filters, and preview transitions

## 12. Responsive art direction

### Desktop

Use cinematic imagery, asymmetric layouts, paired content and visual fields, and a side-by-side concierge preview.

### Tablet

Preserve the editorial grid while simplifying navigation and reducing extreme whitespace. Plans and comparisons remain directly manipulable.

### Mobile

Prioritize one idea per viewport, intentional crops, concise copy, comfortable touch targets, and a stable action hierarchy. The concierge preview becomes an accessible bottom sheet or full-screen detail view.

## 13. Prototype disclaimer

While temporary reference material is in use, preview environments must display:

> Development concept using temporary reference material. Project details and media require owner confirmation before production publication.

Do not overlay this disclaimer onto every image. Place it persistently but unobtrusively in the preview header or footer and repeat it near downloadable or commercially sensitive content.

## 14. Avoid list

- Copying the reference website layout or visual styling
- Generic black-and-gold luxury templates
- Glassmorphism over detailed property imagery
- Oversized promotional counters without evidence
- Too many card grids or inconsistent corner radii
- Autoplay carousels that hide information
- Aggressive lead-capture pop-ups
- Artificial scarcity, investment guarantees, or unsupported superlatives
- Generated imagery represented as a completed real-world feature
- Low-contrast typography over photographs
- Desktop interactions that rely on hover alone
