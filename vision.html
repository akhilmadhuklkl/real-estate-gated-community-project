A neighbourhood,
not just an address.

The concept begins with a simple idea: homes should protect privacy without isolating people. Villas face quieter internal streets while gardens, paths and shared spaces make everyday encounters natural.

Architecture uses shaded edges, warm materials and framed views to create a contemporary identity suited to its landscape.
